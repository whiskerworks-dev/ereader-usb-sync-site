Accessibility · iPhone and iPad

Clear steps.
Readable text.

E-reader USB Sync supports larger text and uses words and symbols alongside colour to explain what is happening.

Make the text larger

The app follows your iPhone or iPad text-size setting, including accessibility sizes above 200%. To adjust it, open your device’s Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text. Turn on Larger Accessibility Sizes if you need more options.

Book details, instructions, progress and results grow with your setting. Screens scroll so that longer text and actions remain reachable. Large book covers are not required to identify or choose a book: titles and file details are shown separately.

Understand status without colour

Selected books have checkmarks. Copying, completed, skipped and failed books have written status labels; progress includes counts. Folder and connection messages use words and symbols, so colour is not the only way to understand the next step.

Other accessibility features

We are continuing to evaluate VoiceOver, Voice Control, dark appearance, contrast and reduced motion. We have not yet declared full support for those features. The app does not play audio or video that needs captions or audio descriptions.

Ask for help

If a screen or action is difficult to use, email whiskerworks@nxtlab.dev. Tell us which screen you were using, your device and the accessibility setting involved. You do not need to send books, passwords or private screenshots.
